# Welcome

Welcome to probably my most extensive article/post yet. I'm currently working my way through *Digital Design and Computer Architecture by Harris & Harris* in attempt to learn, well, digital design and computer architecture!

This article serves as the ramblings and thoughts of someone attempting to write their own RISC-V processor, while also attempting to help you write your own RISC-V processor. I find explaining my thoughts out-loud or even writing them out
helps me expose gaps in my knowledge.

Yes, the goal with this article is to document my process of going from a single-cycle to a pipelined multi-cycle RISC-V processor with some fun stuff along the way, but if you want the full experience, I urge you to buy a copy of [Digital Design and Computer Architecture](https://www.amazon.com/Digital-Design-Computer-Architecture-RISC-V-dp-0128200642/dp/0128200642?me=).
It's well worth the money.
